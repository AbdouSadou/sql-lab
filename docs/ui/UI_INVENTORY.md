# UI inventory

Every interactive element of the interface as it was before the redesign (commit 0a088a7), its handler, the tests that pin it, and its home in the target design of `docs/design/SQL Lab UI and UX Plan.md` (section 5). Phase 1 of the plan writes it; Phase 2 ticks the **Done** column of each row once the element works in the new shell, in its new home or, where that home belongs to a later phase (toasts, dialogs and the empty state in Phase 4, the exercise prompt in Phase 5), in the place the row's note names until then; a removal is ticked once its function lives where the row says. Phase 3 moved the editor's rows (22, 23, 24, 26 to 29, 31 to 35 and 49) to CodeMirror, their final home; Phase 4 moved the rows of the result area, the toasts and the dialogs (14 to 17, 21, 42 to 48, 51, 54 to 71, 77, 79, 82, 83, 141 to 144, 146, 148, 149 and 153 to 156) to theirs; Phase 5 moved the exercise mode's (89, 101 and 106 to 110: the hint button that counts, Previous and Next, the feedback banner, the Expected and Yours view), so no row is interim any more. Phase 6 added the drawer under 1100 px (row 114) and restyled the About page (157 to 164), whose inline back() became js/lab/static-page.js, and the evidence check page's header (165, 175). Nothing may be lost: a row is only ticked when what its details ask to keep still holds.

How it was made: six readers each went through one area of the code and the tests, and a seventh searched every listener, `tabindex`, `role`, dialog and key handler for anything they missed (it found two). An independent review then corrected the homes of rows 39, 40, 72, 73, 81, 82, 121, 141 and 142. Screenshots of the same interface are in `docs/ui/before/`.

Vocabulary of the homes: "Top bar", "Sidebar" (Schema, Exercises and History tabs), "Editor toolbar", "Editor", "Results pane", "Exercise prompt panel", "Status bar", "Toast" and "Dialog" are the regions of plan sections 4 and 5; "Removed" names a duplicate the plan removes and where its function goes; "Unchanged" is a page outside the app shell.
